First-time Spotify setup (once, needs Spotify Premium)
  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard, log in, Create app.
  2. Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:5050/spotify/callback (exactly), tick Web API, Save.
  3. Copy the Client ID into the box above, press Connect Spotify, log in and Agree.
  4. Keep the Spotify app open on this laptop. For offline, download your playlists in the Spotify app.
